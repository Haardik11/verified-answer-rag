Quarterly Revenue Notes

Q3 revenue came in at 4.2 million dollars, a 12 percent increase over the
prior year quarter. Management attributes this primarily to expansion in
the enterprise segment, partially offset by a slight slowdown in the SMB
tier due to seasonal purchasing patterns.

Looking ahead to Q4, guidance remains cautious given macro uncertainty,
though the pipeline of enterprise deals in late-stage negotiation suggests
upside potential if even half of them close within the quarter.
